title: "Halloween Horrors: A Saturday Night Altium Session"
date: "November 1, 2026"
---

On Halloween night, I sat in a small Iraqi cafe in Anaheim with my friend, attempting to make progress on a task we had been putting off all week. We had been asked by our Micromouse team lead to wire up a battery connector and produce a schematic and footprint in Altium that could be integrated into our larger "mouse" PCB. The effort was fueled by matcha and a Biscoff tres leches cake while rain poured outside, a sign that autumn has (finally!) arrived in Southern California. 

I soon encountered my first Halloween horror: I had no mouse with me. Attempting to use Altium with only a trackpad was a fool's endeavour. But I had no choice. Over the next few hours, I fought my trackpad's unpredictable panning and played a game of hide-and-seek with my schematic. 

The second horror of Halloween night came in my realization that I had let my Altium skills rust for far too long. I also did not entirely remember what our task was. After a few hours of Googling and a brief call with our lead, I emerged with a simple schematic and a new task 🙃

Oh well, the footprint will have to wait until Monday.
